Privacy Policy for Mega Music Player

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SZ Studio ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with your use of our mobile application, Mega Music Player (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding all local and online data interactions.

1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to Mega Music Player. Our application functions both as a local, offline audio player and as an online streaming client for open-access music and public video content. By downloading, installing, or using Mega Music Player, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect and Process

We do not require user accounts, registration, or login credentials to use Mega Music Player. However, to deliver streaming features, measure app stability, and support ongoing development, certain technical and usage data is processed:

  • Personal Information: We do not directly collect or store personal contact details such as your name, email address, phone number, or passwords.
  • Audio Files and Local Storage: The App accesses your device's local storage solely to read, index, and play audio files stored on your hardware. Your local media files remain strictly on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Usage Analytics (Firebase Analytics): We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App (such as screen views, session duration, and feature engagement). Firebase automatically collects non-personal technical metadata including device model, operating system version, app version, Firebase Installation ID, and coarse geographic location (country/region derived from your IP address).
  • Anonymous Community Telemetry: To power community-driven music charts, the App may transmit anonymous, aggregated playback counts associated with a randomly generated local identifier. This data cannot be used to personally identify you.
  • Advertising Identifiers (Google AdMob): To keep the App free, we may display native advertisements powered by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process your device's Android Advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction diagnostics to serve and measure advertisements.

3. Permissions Requested by the App

To deliver local and online multimedia playback, the App requests the following Android system permissions:

  • Storage / Media Access: Used strictly to scan local directories and play your local audio files.
  • Audio Focus / Foreground Services: Used to manage playback states (such as pausing audio during phone calls) and display persistent playback controls in the notification shade.
  • Internet and Network State Access: Required to stream music and video content from third-party APIs, load community charts, transmit anonymous analytics, and display advertisements.

We never use these permissions to access your contacts, precise GPS coordinates, camera, or microphone.

4. Third-Party Services and APIs

Mega Music Player integrates third-party APIs and SDKs to provide music discovery, video playback, analytics, and advertising. Your use of these features is also subject to the respective privacy policies and terms of those providers:

  • YouTube API Services: The video section of the App uses YouTube API Services to display and play video content. By using the video features in Mega Music Player, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's collection and handling of data through YouTube services are governed by the Google Privacy Policy. We do not access, store, or harvest your personal YouTube account history or credentials.
  • Audius & Jamendo: Online music discovery and audio streaming are powered by official developer APIs from Audius and Jamendo.
  • Google Firebase & AdMob: Analytics and advertising services are provided by Google. You can learn more about how Google processes data from partner apps at Google Privacy & Terms. You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by resetting or deleting your Advertising ID in your Android device's Privacy settings.

5. Data Security

All network communications between Mega Music Player and third-party APIs (including Firebase Analytics, YouTube, Audius, Jamendo, and AdMob) are encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. Local media indexes and playback preferences remain stored securely on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Mega Music Player does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that personal information has been inadvertently collected through a third-party service, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new App features or changes in regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this publicly accessible page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
